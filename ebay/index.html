What the calculator covers
Built around eBay's UK business fee structure
Final value fee Category-dependent percentage applied to the sale amount, with the current £0.30 / £0.40 per-order fee.
Regulatory operating fee 0.35% of the total sale amount, before VAT.
International fee 1.05%, 1.80% or 2.00% depending on the buyer's overseas destination.
Listing and optional fees Insertion fee plus any selected optional charges, such as a second category, Promoted Listings or Reserve Price.
Monthly eBay Shop subscriptions (not included in the sale total) Current UK business Shop prices: Basic £27/month, Featured £77/month, Anchor £437/month. These are fixed monthly costs rather than per-sale fees.
Other specialist fees (not included by default) Classified Ad listing fees and other account-specific services can use separate fee structures. Check the official eBay fee page when using those formats.
